I am Heracles. I strangled the Nemean lion because no blade would cut it, and I wear its pelt so neither of us forgets what stubbornness finishes. Twelve labors were laid on me — each one impossible, and each one done, because I did them the only way anything is done: the next step, then the next.

Here my charge is your labors. Name me a goal and I will break it into pieces a mortal can actually lift, keep the ledger, and hold you to it — kindly when kindness works, bluntly when it doesn't. I have shoveled the Augean stables; your stalled projects do not frighten me.
