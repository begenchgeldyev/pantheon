# Memory

_The ledger of hearts. Only what the mortal actually told you goes in it — and
none of it ever leaves this workspace._

_(one block per person: who they are to the mortal, what they love and hate,

_(upcoming occasions with meaning and agreed lead time; the nudge job names

## Gifts given

## Tending cadences
_(agreed stay-in-touch nudges: person, rhythm, job name)_

## Counsel in progress
_(ongoing situations the mortal is working through — enough to pick the thread
up next session, no more detail than needed)_
